8. SCREENS 3 AND 4 — PREVIEWED, DISCRETE TOUCH-ZONE CHANGES

Match 03_transition_warning.png and 04_target_mode.png.
This interaction OVERRIDES older requirements about balloon-like moving targets.

CURRENT ZONE:
A stationary rounded rectangle, solid luminous cyan border, dark translucent
fill, and a readable TAP HERE or CURRENT AREA label.

NEXT ZONE:
A stationary rounded rectangle at its future position, dashed magenta border,
subtle purple fill, and a NEXT AREA label. Preview the EXACT future bounds,
including its size. A small arrow can connect the zones as in the reference.

Header during preview:
NEXT TAP AREA IN 3 TAPS
then 2 TAPS, then 1 TAP.

Behavior:
- Keep the existing area active while the preview is visible.
- The preview is NOT a valid target yet. Touching it follows the mode's existing
  outside-target rule; do not count it as a valid tap.
- Start a preview at least three accepted taps before a shift.
- Also require at least 600ms of visible preview, measured in active game time.
- Decrement only on accepted taps in the current area.
- If the count reaches zero before 600ms, keep the old area active and show
  NEXT AREA READY. Switch on the first valid tap at/after the minimum duration.
- Count that triggering tap once against the OLD geometry, then atomically
  activate the previewed rectangle for subsequent inputs.
- New area appears in place with a brief activation glow. It does not travel,
  slide, bounce, follow a path, or get tweened across the screen.
- Match the target-mode screenshot with a bright magenta activation burst;
  settle the solid active outline to cyan for ongoing current/next clarity.
- For a configurable 200ms handoff grace, either the old or new rectangle may
  accept a tap. Show the fading old outline while it is still accepted.
- After grace, only the new area is valid. Each pointer-down can score ONCE,
  including inside overlapping rectangles or during the handoff.
- Every accepted tap resets the usual 1.5-second inactivity timer. Decorative
  animations and previews must not reset it or fabricate taps.

The dashed/solid border and labels must distinguish states without color alone.
Keep both labels legible and avoid overlaps with HUD/milestone/ad regions.

Difficulty can increase through smaller fixed boxes, more frequent shifts,
farther-apart positions, or split fixed boxes in Chaos. All upcoming active
geometry must be previewed. Keep a practical initial minimum target size around
88x88 logical pixels, larger where needed for usability.

Use the SAME rectangle/path data for rendering, preview, and hit testing. Keep
coordinates local to the bounded gameplay arena. Decorative glow is outside the
hitbox; it must not silently enlarge/shrink accepted input.

Do not change target size or position while it is the active area. No continuous
target motion, including during bosses. Recalculate safely on resize/orientation
changes; pause/re-arm rather than moving a target under an ongoing touch.

Implement these as states of the existing gameplay screen. Casual remains a
full-field mode; Campaign and Chaos use the fixed-zone transition system.

16. MOTION, INPUT, HAPTICS, AND AD LAYOUT

Button press: approximately 80ms compression, 120ms return.
Screen transition: approximately 180-250ms fade/short displacement.
Tap ring/burst: approximately 180-450ms, exact input position.
Zone activation: approximately 120-180ms glow; no positional interpolation.
Background illustration motion: slow and subtle, with a static fallback.

Reduce Motion removes shakes, parallax, and displacement. Reduce Flashing uses
steady glows/smooth fades instead of flashes. Do not use high-contrast full-screen
strobing. Stronger effects must never obscure current/next targets or the timer.
Rate-limit haptics and bound particles. Provide a no-haptics fallback on web.

Decorative overlays must not intercept input. Modal pause/settings/game-over
screens must block gameplay intentionally and pause the run. Use actual pointer
DOWN events, not touch-hold, hover, particle events, or duplicate input handlers.

Layout the game as separate bounded HUD, arena, milestone footer, and banner
regions. Fit the 320x50 ad INSIDE its reserved region, centered; leave a deliberate
noninteractive divider/gap from rapid-tap controls. Keep safe-area insets separate
from the ad height. Do not stretch the ad to fill the screen width.

Never draw effects above a real ad, style a game control as an ad close button,
or count ad interaction as gameplay. Use a labeled placeholder in UI previews;
use real ad-service availability in production. No ad fill must not cause the
arena or hitboxes to jump during a run. Preserve platform-specific ad adapters.

A. Inspect repo/reference; save UI specification, crop references, create tokens.
B. Build production art and shared components; finish Main Menu first.
C. Finish gameplay HUD, arena, milestone footer, and discrete target previews.
D. Finish Game Over, Campaign, and Chaos entry.

Run Android builds and iOS simulator/unsigned builds where tooling permits.
Record actual command output, failures, and external blockers. Never label a
platform tested unless it was tested.

Test at minimum:
- Every visible enabled control has its intended action.
- HUD and banner touches never score.
- Timer pauses in menus/system interruptions and resumes deliberately.
- Preview bounds equal the next active bounds.
- Preview remains visible before activation; no moving-target tween exists.
- Triggering tap is evaluated once against the old zone.
- Handoff grace accepts only the specified areas and expires correctly.
- Target/effect geometry never intrudes into HUD or ad space.
- Settings persist and reduced-effects modes change rendering.
- Retry/revive do not create duplicate game instances or stale animations.
- Fixture data cannot write to real profiles or appear as live leaderboards.

Delivery:
- Branch and commit identifiers.
- Files/assets created or changed.
- Ten actual screen captures and the rendered contact sheet.
- A short recording of preview -> switch -> active tapping.
- Screen-by-screen visual comparison and unresolved artwork differences.
- Analyzer/test/build results with platform-specific limitations.
- Backend integrations actually connected versus externally blocked.

Finish with real, interactive Flutter UI that visibly matches the supplied design.
